## How this plays
This is a palaces-only implementation, which means you don't interact with the calendar or are really outside of the palaces / mementos (asides from the introduction - up until you unlock Ann). Previously the way to warp was to click the mementos gate which meant that would need to play until mementos to unlock the warp / shops, but with the F9 menu, you can warp earlier (how much earlier is still something that needs to be tested to find out because of story flags).

## Features and available checks

The default configuration provides **231 checks**:

- **155 named Palace chest checks**, with vanilla rewards replaced by their scouted AP items.
- **Eight calling-card checks** and **eight Palace-completion checks**.
- **20 numbered AP shop checks** by default, configurable from 0 to 500. The default price range is 1,000–10,000 yen. Shop checks unlock across the eight Palace-access tiers.
- **40 Mementos chest checks** by default: five checks in each of eight chest-bearing Paths, configurable from 1 to 20 per Path.
- **232 optional Persona-registration checks** through Compendium Sanity. Enabling these increases the default total to **463 checks**.

## Available goals
- One or multiple **Palace-clear goals**: Kamoshida, Madarame, Kaneshiro, Futaba, Okumura, Sae, Shido or Mementos. The default is **Shido**.
- Compendium completion, which can be used alone or alongside Palace goals. The default is **disabled**.

## Gameplay features
- Clearing a palace will send all of its chest checks immediately.
- A game-native **F9 hub** with Connection, Shop and Warps menus, also accessible through the Mementos entrance ticket gates. Host, slot and password can be entered using keyboard input.
- Palace actions to **enter a Palace**, **send its calling card**, **disable its calling-card route**, or return to the previous menu.
- **Progressive Palace Access** and **Progressive Calling Card** items, with an option to use individual per-Palace access items instead.
- **Eight Progressive Mementos Access tiers** allowing you to warp to it.
- One starting **Progressive Palace Access** and one starting **Progressive Mementos Access**, removed from the randomized pool.
- **Six party recruitment items** for Yusuke, Makoto, Haru, Futaba, Akechi and Sumire
- **37 Party Level Up items** in +1, +3 and +5 variants, providing **81 shared levels** in total. Recruited members receive the accumulated level backlog, with a level cap of 99.
- **70 uniformly selected filler rewards**: 40 TV-shopping bundles plus chest consumables, clinic medicine, melee weapons, guns and armor, applied to inventory at the hub.
- Scouted AP item and recipient information in chest windows and the AP shop.
- **DeathLink** on a party wipe. Receive percentage damage to Joker and the active combat party the default is **30%**, with living members left at at least 1 HP.
